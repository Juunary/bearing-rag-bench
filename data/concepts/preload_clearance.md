Internal clearance is the play between the rolling elements and the raceways; preload is a negative clearance applied by an axial force or by the fit. Clearance after mounting is reduced by interference fits and by temperature differences between the rings. Correct clearance or preload affects load distribution, stiffness, running accuracy, noise and life.
