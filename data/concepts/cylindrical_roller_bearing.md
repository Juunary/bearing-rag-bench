A cylindrical roller bearing uses rollers in line contact with the raceways, giving a high radial load capacity and little axial capacity. The roller ends are profiled (crowned or relieved) to avoid edge stress. Full-complement versions without a cage carry more load but are more prone to roller slip at low load.
