Wear monitoring estimates the condition of a sliding or rolling contact without disassembly, by examining the lubricant and the machine's behaviour. Wear particle analysis of oil samples (ferrography, spectrometric analysis, particle counting) identifies the amount, size and shape of debris and therefore the wear mechanism; sensors in the bearing can track wear directly. It is used to plan maintenance before a bearing fails.
