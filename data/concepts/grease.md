Lubricating grease is a base oil held in a thickener, usually a metal soap such as lithium soap or a non-soap thickener such as polyurea or clay. The thickener fixes the operating temperature range and resistance to water, while the base oil type and viscosity set the lubricant film. Additives give extreme-pressure, anti-wear and corrosion protection; grease life falls quickly above the thickener's dropping point.
