The cage keeps the rolling elements evenly spaced and prevents them from touching each other, guiding them through the unloaded zone. It is made of stamped steel, brass or polymer and is guided by the rolling elements or by a ring shoulder. Cage wear, fracture and pocket friction are common causes of bearing noise and failure.
