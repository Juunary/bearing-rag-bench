An angular contact ball bearing has raceways offset so that the balls transmit load at a contact angle, which lets it carry combined radial and axial load in one direction. A larger contact angle supports more axial load. Such bearings are usually mounted in pairs and preloaded, which stiffens the arrangement and keeps the balls under control at high speed.
