Fretting is wear and oxidation between two surfaces that move against each other by very small amounts, such as a bearing ring that is loosely fitted in its housing or a shaft under vibration. The debris is a reddish-brown oxide (fretting corrosion, fit rust). In bearings it appears on seats with a loose fit and at standstill under vibration, and it can start fatigue cracks.
