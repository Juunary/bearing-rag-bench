The sealing lip is the elastomer edge of a radial shaft seal that touches the shaft along a narrow contact line. Its two contact angles create an asymmetric pressure profile that pumps oil back toward the oil side. Lip wear, hardening and loss of spring force change this contact and can cause leakage.
