Hertzian contact stress is the pressure in the small elastic contact area between a rolling element and a raceway, or between any two curved bodies pressed together. It rises with load and with the curvature of the contacting surfaces and reaches values of a few thousand megapascals in rolling bearings. The maximum Hertz stress is the main variable in bearing fatigue life models.
