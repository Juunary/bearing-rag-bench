Brinelling is permanent indentation of a raceway by the rolling elements, caused by a static or shock load that exceeds the material's yield strength, for example when a bearing is dropped or pressed in through the wrong ring. False brinelling looks similar but is wear from small oscillations without rotation, such as vibration during transport or standstill. Both produce periodic marks that later cause noise and fatigue.
