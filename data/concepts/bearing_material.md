Rolling bearing rings and elements are made of through-hardened high-carbon chromium steel (AISI 52100 / 100Cr6), case-carburized steel for shock loads, or high-temperature tool steels such as M-50 for aircraft engines. Vacuum melting (VIM-VAR) removes inclusions and raises fatigue life, which is credited with material life factors. Silicon nitride ceramic balls in hybrid bearings reduce mass, friction and electrical conduction.
