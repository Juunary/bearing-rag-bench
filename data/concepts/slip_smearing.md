Roller slip occurs when rolling elements do not rotate at the kinematically correct speed, typically at low load or high acceleration, so they slide on the raceway. Sliding under a thin lubricant film tears the surfaces and transfers material, a damage called smearing. Full-complement and lightly loaded cylindrical roller bearings are especially affected.
