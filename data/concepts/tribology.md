Tribology is the science of surfaces in relative motion: friction, wear and lubrication. Friction is described by a coefficient that depends on the materials, the surface state and the lubrication regime; wear mechanisms include abrasion, adhesion (galling, scuffing, seizure), surface fatigue and tribochemical reaction. A tribological system is characterised by the two bodies, the intermediate medium and the operating conditions.
