A plain bearing carries the shaft on a sliding surface instead of rolling elements, so the load is spread over an area rather than concentrated at points or lines. It can run with a hydrodynamic oil film, with boundary lubrication, or dry with self-lubricating materials such as plastics. Plain bearings tolerate shock loads and contamination well and are simple and inexpensive, but friction and wear depend strongly on lubrication.
