A tapered roller bearing has conical rollers and raceways whose extended surfaces meet at a common point on the bearing axis, so the rollers roll without sliding. It supports heavy combined radial and axial load in one direction and is normally used in opposed pairs whose clearance or preload is set during assembly, for example in vehicle wheel hubs and gearboxes.
