A radial shaft seal is a ring with an elastomer sealing lip that closes the gap between a rotating shaft and a stationary housing, keeping lubricant in and contaminants out. The lip is pressed against the shaft by a garter spring and runs on a thin lubricant film. Its sealing behaviour depends on the lip, the lubricant and the shaft surface it runs on.
