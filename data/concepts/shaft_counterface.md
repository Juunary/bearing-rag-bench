The shaft counterface is the surface of the shaft on which a sealing lip runs. Its roughness, hardness, lead (a helical machining pattern), runout and eccentricity determine whether the lip can follow the shaft and whether the lubricant film and pumping effect work as intended. The surface is usually ground without lead to a specified roughness.
