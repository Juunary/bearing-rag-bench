The bearing fit is the dimensional relationship between a bearing ring and its seat on the shaft or in the housing. The ring that rotates relative to the load direction needs an interference (press or shrink) fit so that it cannot turn on its seat; the stationary ring may have a transition or light clearance fit. A fit that is too loose lets the ring creep and fret, while one that is too tight removes the internal clearance.
