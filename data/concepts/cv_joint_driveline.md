A constant-velocity joint transmits torque between shafts at a changing angle without the speed fluctuation of a simple universal joint, and is used on the drive shafts of front-wheel-drive and independent-suspension vehicles. The joint runs in grease inside a flexible boot; a torn boot lets grease out and dirt and water in, which quickly wears the joint. The driveline also includes propeller shafts, universal joints and the differential.
