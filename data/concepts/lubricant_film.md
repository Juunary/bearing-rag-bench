The lubricant film separates the two surfaces of a contact; its thickness depends on viscosity, speed, load and geometry. The Stribeck curve relates friction to these variables through the regimes of boundary, mixed and full-film (hydrodynamic or elastohydrodynamic) lubrication. In rolling bearings the film is elastohydrodynamic, and the ratio of film thickness to surface roughness is used as a life factor.
