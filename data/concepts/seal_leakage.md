Leakage of a radial shaft seal means lubricant passes the lip contact toward the air side. An intact seal pumps oil back toward the oil side; leakage appears when this conveying effect is overwhelmed or lost, for example through lip wear, a damaged or incorrectly machined shaft surface, excessive runout, or lip lift-off at high speed.
