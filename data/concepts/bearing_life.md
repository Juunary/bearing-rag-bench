Bearing fatigue life is the number of revolutions or hours a bearing runs before rolling-contact fatigue damages a raceway or rolling element. The L10 (rating) life is the life that 90 percent of a group of identical bearings reach; it is calculated from the dynamic load rating and the load with the Lundberg-Palmgren relation and adjusted with life factors for material, lubrication and reliability. Actual field life is also limited by causes other than fatigue.
