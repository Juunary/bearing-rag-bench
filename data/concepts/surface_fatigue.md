Rolling contact fatigue is the cracking of a raceway or rolling element surface under repeated Hertzian contact stress. Cracks start at or below the surface, often at inclusions or dents, and grow until material breaks out as spalls or pits. It is the failure mode that bearing life ratings describe, as distinct from wear, corrosion or damage from mishandling.
